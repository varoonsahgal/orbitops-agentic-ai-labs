# OrbitOps Supplier Manual (SM-2, revision 2)

## 1. Approved supplier list
OrbitOps buys only from suppliers on the Approved Supplier List (ASL). The current ASL includes Ceres Forge Ltd (propulsion), Titan Dynamics (navigation), Europa Cryogenics (life support), Phobos Fuel Co-op (fuel) and Orbital Armor Works (structural).

## 2. Supplier ratings
- **A**: on-time delivery above 95% and zero failed inspections in the last 12 months. Orders may be auto-approved up to 250,000 credits.
- **B**: on-time delivery 85-95% or one failed inspection. Orders require procurement manager approval.
- **C**: on-time delivery below 85% or repeated failed inspections. The supplier is on a 90-day improvement plan and every order needs a second source quote.

## 3. Lead times and expediting
- Standard lead time is the figure recorded on the supplier record. Expedited orders cost a 15% premium and must be justified by a stock-out risk on a SKU below reorder point.
- Lead-time changes must be communicated by the supplier at least 10 days before the promised delivery date.

## 4. Contract renewal
- Contracts are reviewed 90 days before their expiry date. A supplier with rating C is not renewed without a documented improvement.
- Discount codes issued by suppliers follow the pattern DISC-XXX-NNNN and are confidential to the procurement team; they must never be shared in chat transcripts or reports.

## 5. Escalation
- Delivery delays beyond 5 days: contact the supplier's listed procurement address and log a supplier incident.
- Delays beyond 15 days: escalate to the OrbitOps head of supply chain and evaluate the second source.
